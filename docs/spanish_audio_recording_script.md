## 📋 Recording Guidelines

### Technical Requirements
- **File Format:** MP3
- **Sample Rate:** 44.1 kHz (or higher)
- **Bit Rate:** 128 kbps minimum
- **Channels:** Mono or Stereo (mono preferred for smaller file size)
- **Duration:** 
  - Vocabulary words: 1-2 seconds each (clear, natural pronunciation)
  - Lesson phrases: 2-4 seconds each (natural conversational pace)
- **File Naming:** Use the exact filename specified for each word (case-sensitive)

### Recording Environment
- **Location:** Quiet room with minimal background noise
- **Microphone:** Use a quality microphone (USB or XLR preferred)
- **Distance:** 6-12 inches from microphone
- **Posture:** Sit comfortably, speak naturally
- **Pace:** Speak at a natural, clear pace (not rushed, not too slow)

### Pronunciation Guidelines
- Use **neutral Spanish** (preferably from Spain or Latin America - specify your region)
- Pronounce words clearly and naturally
- Use standard pronunciation (avoid regional accents that may confuse learners)
- Each word should be spoken **once** per recording
- Pause briefly (0.5 seconds) before and after speaking the word
- Do NOT add any background music, effects, or additional words

### Quality Checklist
Before submitting, verify each recording:
- ✅ Clear pronunciation
- ✅ No background noise
- ✅ Consistent volume across all files
- ✅ Correct file name
- ✅ File plays correctly
- ✅ Duration is appropriate (words: 1-2s, phrases: 2-4s)

## 🎤 Recording Tips

1. **Warm Up:** Practice saying a few words before recording to get comfortable
2. **Consistency:** Try to maintain the same tone, volume, and pace across all recordings
3. **Take Breaks:** Record in batches (e.g., all nouns, then all verbs) to maintain quality
4. **Review:** Listen back to each recording before moving to the next
5. **Re-record if needed:** Don't hesitate to re-record if you're not satisfied with the quality
6. **Natural Speech:** Speak naturally - these are words learners will hear in real conversations
